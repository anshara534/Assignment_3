Language

Punjabi is the main language associated with Punjab. It is spoken in different dialects and has a rich tradition of poetry, folk stories, and songs. In Pakistan, Punjabi is commonly written in the Shahmukhi script, while in Indian Punjab, Gurmukhi is widely used

Traditional Dress

Traditional Punjabi clothing is colorful and comfortable. Men commonly wear shalwar kameez, sometimes with a turban or pagri. Women traditionally wear shalwar kameez, often decorated with embroidery, along with colorful dupattas. Phulkari, a famous Punjabi embroidery style, is particularly important in Punjabi culture.

Food

Punjabi cuisine is known for its rich flavors and generous use of spices, butter, and dairy products. Popular foods include sarson da saag, makki di roti, lassi, chana, and various types of kebabs and meat dishes. Food is an important part of Punjabi hospitality, and guests are often offered plenty to eat.

Festivals

Punjabis celebrate many religious, cultural, and seasonal festivals. Basant has historically been associated with spring and kite flying in parts of Punjab. Baisakhi is an important harvest festival, particularly in Indian Punjab. Eid celebrations are especially important among Pakistani Punjabis, while Sikh and Hindu communities celebrate their respective religious festivals.

Music and Dance

Punjabi music is famous for its energetic rhythms and strong connection to everyday life. Traditional instruments include the dhol, tumbi, algoza, and chimta. Popular dances include bhangra and giddha. Folk songs often describe love, marriage, farming, separation, bravery, and village life.
